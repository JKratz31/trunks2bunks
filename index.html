Frequently asked

Questions? Answered.

Search or browse. Still stuck? Email or text us. We answer around the clock.

Registration deadline. Same Day Return is limited, so if it matters to you, register as early as you can.

Is there a deadline to register?

Yes. Please register before April 22nd. Same Day Return spots are limited, so if that's a priority for your family, register as soon as you can.

Do I need to measure or weigh our trunks?

No. We know they're heavy! We don't charge extra for oversized luggage. Just give us as much information as you can when you register so we can plan the best service for you.

How will I know my pick-up date?

We assign a pick-up time and email it to you 4–6 weeks in advance. Specific shipping dates and steps all come by email.

When do trunks come home?

In August, every trunk is returned home within one week of the last day of camp. If you need it faster, ask about our limited Same Day Return option.

What if we want to ship items besides trunks or duffel bags?

Sports equipment and other items outside the trunks must be boxed or inside another bag (like a hockey bag). These aren't part of registration. Email info@trunks2bunk.com for a quote after you've registered your luggage, and we'll do our best to fit them in.

Campers can also bring sporting equipment, backpacks, chairs, and fragile items to the bus stop. When the buses roll into camp, counselors unload the storage underneath and help campers with their things.

What if we're not home on pick-up day and can't leave our trunks out?

Email info@trunks2bunk.com or call (413) 344-8155 and tell us where the trunks will be. Some good alternate spots:

  • A neighbor's home
  • A locked garage (call us with an entry code, or open it from an app on your phone)
  • Another camp family's home in your area
Do I need to tell you about changes to our order?

Yes. Email info@trunks2bunk.com or text or call (413) 344-8155 to make changes.

  • Address change: email us at least two weeks before your scheduled service. There's no added fee. We just need to coordinate with our drivers.
  • Round Trip ↔ One Way: we'll pro-rate your bill to match.
  • First Session → full summer: we get a report from the Camp Director and update your order for you.
Where should we leave the trunks on pick-up day?

Give as much detail as you can when you register. Drivers carry a copy of your instructions. If you say "front porch," make sure the trunks are on the front porch that day. Ideally, trunks are outside on a front or back porch or in an unlocked garage. If you'll be home, trunks can stay inside and drivers will ring the doorbell.

NYC families: we pick up from your doorman or service entrance unless you tell us otherwise. Trunks must be with the doorman the night before, because pick-ups start very early. Live in a non-doorman building, or your doorman isn't there 24 hours? Let us know and we'll work it out.

Florida families: if you live in a gated community, send thorough instructions, including the community name and any rules for commercial vehicles.

Should I include our neighborhood or development name?

Yes. Include the development or neighborhood name and any instructions we should know, like which entrance to use or entry restrictions. In a gated community, please alert security ahead of time so drivers can get in for pick-up and delivery.

Do we need our own shipping tags?

No. We send you shipping tags and instructions for attaching them. An extra form of ID, like a stenciled last name on each trunk, helps drivers and counselors.

For Round Trip service, each trunk has one luggage tag. The tag helps us sort trunks quickly on camp property and load them in the right order for the trip home. Secure it to a handle or metal loop. Don't use the tag to tie zippers together.
